# Inside the terrarium

*A growing wiki for Terrarium: Seasons of Wonder, written by Codex on Luis's behalf.*

The world is more than a collection of rules. A trait depends on the body carrying it, what that body can find, the other creatures around it, and the history of the place. These pages connect those levels and say what the evidence does and does not establish.

Start with [the whole-world guide](world-guide.md), or read [a short history of questions and turning points](history.md).

For quick orientation and reporting help, see [questions a visitor might ask](questions.md).

For the ideas Luis has actually explored with the steward, read [concepts behind the world](concepts/README.md): from energy and physiology to geographic isolation, geology, and computational constraints.

For what can be reconstructed from files, fossils, reports, and commits, read [the surviving archive](archive.md).

## Coverage and gaps

The trait inventory is broader than the completed reference. Detailed parasitism, fat/energy accounting, grass/root dynamics, behaviour-code variation, crowding, and species-splitting accounts remain to be written. Unique giants also need their own treatment. Two species accounts and one species-only map are a beginning, not a complete encyclopedia.

New pages should connect a mechanism to its conditions, observable quantities, exceptions, and missing evidence—not merely add definitions. The wider [wiki plan](../WIKI-PLAN.md) sets the coverage; [Codex's backlog](../CODEX.md) tracks actionable work.

## Two clocks to keep in mind

A rule explanation refers to a source revision. An observation refers to a particular world and season. They can differ: historical reports may use rules that have since changed, and a fresh world may not activate the saved world's rollouts.

The [publication record](../PUBLICATION.md) maps the local revision used for the first wiki checks to its equivalent in the public history.

Start a separate world with the [running guide](../RUNNING.md), or explore the [reports](../../reports/status/README.md). The reports are historical material, not a live census.
