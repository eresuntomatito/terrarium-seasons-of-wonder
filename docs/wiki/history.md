# A history of questions, not a march toward a goal

Written by Codex on eresuntomatito's behalf. This is a short reading route through the steward's published reports, not an independent reconstruction of every season. Luis describes the project's purpose as curiosity and the joy of building a world, rather than making a useful product or directing a predetermined outcome.

The reports mix observations, interpretations, tests, and changes to the rules. The table separates those categories where possible. Its numbers and descriptions belong to their dated reports; they are not a current census.

| Moment | Question or intervention | What the historical record says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seasons 761–797: a narrow world | Could a largely grass-specialist family find other ways to live? | The [761 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-15_s761.md) describes near-twin grass eaters. The [797 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-15_s797.md) records regional divisions and the loss of the omorush meat line. More named species did not mean a complex food chain. |
| Seasons 789–874: not every plausible addition helps | A stomach proposal was tested; later tests investigated plant digestion, carrion, and provisioning. | The [stomach test](../../reports/status/2026-10-15_s789-stomach-test.md) reports no useful effect in the tested world and was not applied. The [835 test](../../reports/status/2026-10-15_s835-way-out-of-grass-test.md) and [874 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-15_s874.md) describe subsequent experiments and rollout. These are different interventions, not one successful stomach feature. |
| Seasons 998–1090: opening hunting, then losing prey | Several barriers to hunting were changed through staged rollouts. | The [998 test](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s998-hunter-roadmap-test.md) records isolated comparisons. The [1021 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1021.md) reports rising predation and extinctions; by [1090](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1090.md), the last prey line was gone and predation had fallen. A mechanism that enables hunting does not guarantee a persistent food chain. |
| Seasons 1163–1260: food and geography become stronger constraints | Grass energy declined; a mountain, plateau, water, and earthquakes were introduced. | The [1163 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1163.md) describes shrinking bodies as grass became leaner. The [orography proposal](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1172-orography-proposal.md), [1224 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1224.md), and [1260 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1260.md) document terrain rollout, water, and quakes. Deliberate world-building and subsequent organism changes should not be conflated. |
| Seasons 1298–1365: shells, water, and new food | How do differing bodies and resource access relate to the places they occupy? | The [1298 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1298.md) contrasts small shelled browsers with larger hunters. The [1365 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1365.md) describes rising swimming in stream-side lines and organisms using the heights. Reported inherited armour is not necessarily effective protection; see [armour](traits/armour.md). |
| Seasons 1371–1396: an apex was not commanded into existence | Later growth, shell-cracking, rare-species room, and apex-niche rules were enabled; a separate terrain redesign followed. | The [1381 checkpoint](../../reports/status/2026-10-16_s1381-apex-checkpoint.md) and [1396 report](../../reports/status/2026-10-17_s1396.md) say the apex rules had not produced an apex. The report instead describes decomposer growth and new splits. The four-land transformation began at 1396; its proposed and observed effects are distinct. |

## Beyond that reporting boundary

Codex's later [season-1445 photograph](../assets/README.md) confirms fully risen terrain, while the [season-1448 mosali atlas](species/atlas.md) records a dated distribution. Neither is a replacement for a full ecological report or evidence that isolation has produced a particular evolutionary outcome. Unpublished live simulation changes are explicitly outside the checked public-source boundary.

## The collaboration is part of the history

Luis chooses questions and conditions; Claude carries the stewardship and much of the implementation and reporting; the local model integration supplies another route for candidate novelty. Codex's documented role includes publication, explanation, and community work, with separate source evidence for a viewer-review contribution. These are roles, not percentages of authorship. The [attributed steward interview](../STEWARD-INTERVIEW.md) explains its evidence and limits; an earlier Codex-era account remains incomplete.
