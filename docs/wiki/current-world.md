## A landscape that offers several kinds of food

The early four-land redesign is now part of a longer landscape history. The continuing world has river channels and highlands, lake country, scrub, and fruit, nut and conifer woods. Trees supply seasonal food and respond to weather; fire, flowers and insects link further processes. Fish provide another feeding opportunity. These are implemented systems; their existence does not mean a specialist population makes sustained use of each one.

The steward specifically confirms that the temporary river-valley ridge and its sealed pocket were **removed at season 2568**. Describing that enclosure as today's geography would be wrong. Leaf browsing and a proposed moving rain belt are not live features. Home-range attraction is also listed as off; smaller body footprints must not be confused with an active home-range rule.

## The question now: lasting differences in how bodies live

The steward's season-2644 status report described eight species living in separate patches, largely returning to grass in plenty. Later plan entries record experiments with access to lake country, small reductions in grass value, and a change to carrion digestion. The woods repeatedly buffered grass reductions by offering browse and fruit. Insect boosts were undone under the tree guard rail; the steward says their settings are back at the originals.

After the carrion-fit change went live at season 2738, the plan records carrion eating rising from 1.2% to 2.9% by season 2742. At season 2802 it records 3.5% carrion and 1.1% fish, with four counted ways of life. By season 2806 carrion was back to 2.4%, with three ways counted. These are the steward's sequential observations, not a controlled demonstration that one rule caused every difference. The screenshot itself shows a grazer-dominated moment.

Since season 2653, the steward's direction is expressed through three measures: eating further up the food chain, distinct species, and computational cost, with population/tree guard rails and no rescues. The latest reply says the distinct-species measure now tests diet or gene differences against the nearest relative. That newer measure is still outside the selected committed source boundary, so the older card in committed `7fa618c` is not today's complete definition. Neither a passing threshold nor a new species name alone establishes biological reproductive isolation or a lasting food web.
