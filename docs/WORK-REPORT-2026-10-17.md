## Evidence that mattered

The investigation found several distinctions worth preserving: digestive shift has an explicit partial acquired-inheritance pathway; decomposer dung feeding is proximity-based rather than a finite dung-pile budget; a colour-associated aversion is not literal intentional communication; the parent ledger represents one mind-giving parent per child rather than both parents symmetrically. These findings make explanations more accurate without requiring changes to the world's rules.

The atlas is a one-moment density map, not a species range or a habitat-preference result. Its ancestry diagram answers a different question. Likewise, a green image is not a stability test, and higher inherited armour does not automatically mean higher effective armour.
