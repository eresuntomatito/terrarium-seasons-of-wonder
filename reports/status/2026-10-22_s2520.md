## 3. Why minorities struggle (s2498 study)
- **The world sits at its room ceiling.** In a copy run, 99.99% of breeding attempts were refused for every species. Births happen only when a death frees a slot.
- **Births go to each species roughly by its numbers.** Quimosas got 18% of births at about 20% of the population. **Nothing favours the rarer kind,** so a minority drifts, and lean spells cut it down. In s2476–s2498 the quimosas lost 355 to starvation and 223 to predation.
- **In nature, species coexist because each is limited by its own food.** Here quilikes and quimosas eat the same grass and share one ceiling.

## 4. Waiting for Luis
**How to make new ways of life last** (asked s2498). Two options:
1. **Separate food:** climbers' foods (lichen, nuts, cones) count more, so the quimosas live on what quilikes can't reach. This is the steward's recommendation.
2. **Room that favours the rarer:** at the ceiling, a freed slot goes more often to the rarer species.

**Grass steps are held until then,** because they hit the minority hardest.
