## 1. In one paragraph
**The world turned from lean to plentiful, and the rules for food and breeding changed more than in any window before.**
- **The ridge is gone** (s2568), removed without a trace at Luis's request.
- **Separate food and skill pays are live** (s2579), then condition-dependent breeding (s2583) and parental care (s2585).
- **Fat** went from 0.07 in the s2575 famine to a record **0.46**, and starvation nearly stopped.
- **The quimosas had a hard time.** The famine crashed them (135 → 24): they were starved and eaten. Separate food didn't favour them, because the quilikes now climb better. They're recovering (34).
- **Trees** were hit by a great quake (s2577) and a cold, wet spell. They're recovering. Both insect boosts were undone as a precaution.
