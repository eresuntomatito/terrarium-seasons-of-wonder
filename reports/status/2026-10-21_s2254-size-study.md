# Size study: what could make body sizes spread apart? (season 2254, Oct 21)

*Written by the world steward, Claude (Opus 5.5), an AI, at Luis's request. Not written by Luis.*

**Luis's order (s2236):** before changing who eats whom or how bodies die, find the pressures that would create **size variation**, so that the split rules (which now also weigh size and skills) can catch real differences. Small fixes found here may be applied right away; bigger ones go to Luis.

**Method:**
- I read every rule that touches size.
- On copies of the live save, I measured what each size class eats and how well it does.
- I tested one small fix (a stronger Bergmann's rule) for 8 seasons against the current rules.

## 1. Today: size is almost neutral
- **Adult sizes** run from 3.8 to 6.6 (p5–p95). The variation is there.
- **But no size does better than another:**

| Adults | Energy share | Stomach fullness | Share climbing | Diet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small third | 0.67 | 0.72 | 0.30 | grass 77%, dung 13%, shoots 10% |
| Middle third | 0.64 | 0.70 | 0.24 | — |
| Big third | 0.68 | 0.65 | 0.16 | grass 78%, dung 12%, shoots 10% |

**Small and big bodies eat exactly the same thing in the same places.** A split needs a group that lives differently *and* is built differently. Today size does neither.

## 2. Why: the size rules cancel out everywhere

| Rule | Favours | How |
|---|---|---|
| M3 stomach ∝ mass | big | holds more grass |
| Upkeep ∝ mass^0.75 (Kleiber) | small | cheaper to run |
| M2 strength ∝ mass^⅔ | big | climbs and swims faster, resists the current |
| Climbing energy ∝ mass | small | every metre up costs the big more |
| Famine store ∝ mass^¼ | big | lasts longer in hunger |
| Thicket drag | small | big bodies slowed in scrub |
| Cold cost ∝ 1/√size, heat ∝ √size (Bergmann) | depends | see §3 |

Every one of these acts **the same way in every place**, and their sum is close to zero. So nothing pushes small bodies one way and big ones another.

## 3. The tested small fix: a stronger Bergmann's rule (rejected)
- **The idea:** make cold costs fall with size (1/size instead of 1/√size), so cold heights favour big bodies and warm lowlands small ones.
- **What happened** (8 seasons, old vs new):
  - **The world's temperature varies mostly in time, not in space.** Eras swing the whole world warm or cold together. Five of the eight seasons had no cold places at all. Across the map at one moment, temperature ranges only 0.32–0.63.
  - **So the fix pushed everyone the same way at the same time.** It **narrowed** sizes (SD 0.85 → 0.62, against 0.71 under the old rules), the opposite of the goal.
- **Not applied; the code was removed.**

## 4. What the world lacks: a place or a food where small bodies win
- **Jarman–Bell (M3) already says** small bodies should live on rich, quick food: fruit, nuts, insects, seeds.
- **But those foods barely exist as food yet:**
  - insects are 0% of anyone's diet;
  - fruit, nuts and cones are ~0%;
  - there are 15 mature trees.
- **So small bodies have nowhere to go but the same grass,** where they're merely average.

**The real lever is a second food niche, rich and patchy, that rewards small size.** With one:
- small bodies would gather where it is, and live differently;
- the niche split and the body test would see them, since size is in both;
- like mates like (size is already in the kin gap) would keep them apart.

## 5. Options (bigger changes, for Luis)

| Option | What it does | Effect on size |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Rich food that rewards small bodies** | Make fruit, nuts and insects a real share of food where trees and flowers are, and let *small* bodies reach it best. For example, small or light bodies climb into trees (they already have the climbing skill), or forage seeds and insects more efficiently per bite | The niche small bodies lack. **Recommended first** |
| **B. Step 4: bodies hold what they cost** | Tissue costs energy to build, paid by parents and while growing, and returns at death | Gives size a real price. Big parents raise costly young slowly, small ones cheap and fast. Classic r/K strategies, and it makes prey real meals later |
| **C. Climbing paid by the metre** | Make the heights clearly favour small, light climbers (energy ∝ mass already; let climbing *speed* not grow with mass) | The heights favour small, the river favours big (strength against the current): two places, two sizes. Small and close to a fix, but it changes M2's physics, so it's Luis's call |
| D. A temperature that differs by place | Stronger cold on the heights all year, not only in cold eras | Would let Bergmann work by place. It changes the climate model |

**Steward's recommendation:**
- **C** is the smallest step and already sits in the physics. Two habitats would favour two sizes.
- **A** is the deepest: a food niche for small bodies.
- **B** follows naturally once sizes differ, because it makes the difference cost something and pay something.

Under Luis's rule, nothing about eating or dying changes until he decides.
