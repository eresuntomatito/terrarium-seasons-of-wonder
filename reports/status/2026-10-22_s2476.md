## 1. In one paragraph
**For the first time in this run, a new species has taken hold: the quimosas, top climbers that live in the sealed river valley.**
- They split at s2447 from 15 quilikes. They're now **116 bodies**, nearly half the world, with 688 born and up to 11 generations.
- **Every body inside the ridge is a quimosa** (57 of 57). The valley has become their home range.
- Two new rules helped new species:
  - **food values** (s2453): foods are worth what they are;
  - **founder groups** (s2463): a split must start as a real group, and close kin are eaten only in hunger.
- **The other splits still fail.** Zugaltys, drofilis, quiraras, ushnamos and figalkes (insect catchers) blended back into the quilikes. The venthukes and quiomnas died out.
- **Fat stays lean** (0.12–0.25), and a cold, lean spell around s2475 cut grass and young trees.

## 3. The quimosas
- **Origin:** 15 top climbers split at s2447, before founder groups existed. They survived grace and kept breeding.
- **Where they live:** the river valley inside the ridge. 78 of 80 lived there at s2471, and they've since spilled out as they grew.
- **How they differ (inside vs outside the valley):**
  - climb 0.60 vs 0.56, gut 0.11 vs 0.09;
  - swim 0.46 vs 0.52, snap 0.025 vs 0.043.

  A climbing, slightly meat-leaning valley kind.
- **Their record so far:** 688 born, 294 starved, 210 eaten, and they have themselves killed 241.
- **Reading:** isolation by the ridge gave a split what founder rules alone don't: a place of its own, where its kin are near and the quilikes are few.
