## 1. In one paragraph
**The climbing worry reversed itself: physics now rewards climbers and swimmers.**
- Last window climbing fell (−0.65 SD). This window it rose **+1.01 SD** (0.443 → 0.514), and **swimming rose +1.55 SD** (0.312 → 0.391), the fastest trait change on record here. The starved and the breeders alike carried *less* of both than today's living (about −0.6 to −0.7).
- The heights and the river now pay, which is what M2 was built for. The earlier fall was the world's first reaction to climbs taking time; it has adapted since.
- **Gut moved toward meat** (+0.45 SD, 0.038 → 0.055) under the new curves.
- **The scrub came back under control,** to ~9–10% of thin cover, and dense thicket fell from 483 to 364. **Mature trees went from 2 to 7.**
- The mixed feeders thinned from 17 to 4, as the browse share settled at 4%.
- Still one species.
