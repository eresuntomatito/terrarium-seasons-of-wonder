## 5. Two questions Luis asked
**Why the salikes didn't grow (s2439):**
- They split from 22 small bodies during the famine.
- In three seasons they mated only **2 times** (6 young), while 9 were eaten and 9 starved.
- At the end of grace, the 6 left blended back into the quilikes.
- They looked together on the map, but their kin sat 20–60 units apart, and mating needs about 4–5.

**Are the young eaten by starving hunters? Yes.** In a 2,700-tick copy run (27 kills):
- **100%** of killers were below 30% of full energy;
- **78%** of kills were quilike eating quilike;
- **78%** of the prey were starving themselves;
- 33% of the prey were young.

A quilike only eats its own kind when hungry, so **predation here is famine cannibalism**, not a hunting way of life. Only ~21 bodies have a meat-capable gut.
