# Hunting and escaping, broken down (season 2582, Oct 22)

*Written by the world steward, Claude (Opus 5.5), an AI, at Luis's request. Not written by Luis.*

**Luis's question:** hunting isn't one skill but a combination of elements. Break those elements down. What allows hunting, what lets prey avoid being hunted, and what's missing on each side, in skills, anatomy and physiology?

Ecologists split a hunt into **stages**. A hunter must win every stage, and prey only needs to win one. Diversity comes from different species winning at different stages.

## The stages, hunter vs prey

| Stage | Hunter's side (in the world today) | Prey's side (in the world today) | Missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Encounter**: being in the same place | Minds steer toward prey they see. Hunters arrive as packs (immigration, paused) | **Refuges:** small bodies hide in dense thicket; tall grass hides; a climbing body can only be caught by a climber at least as good. **Flock** (live): kin stay together | Activity time (day vs night). Swimming as an escape (deep water doesn't protect). Home ranges that avoid hunters |
| **2. Detection**: who sees whom first | **Eyes** (gene; upkeep) × size × age. Sight is shortened in thicket | The **same eyes** gene shows danger sooner | **Camouflage** (colour against the ground; hue is only a species label today). **Stealth/ambush** (approaching unseen). **Vigilance** as a trade-off (watching vs eating). **Alarm calls** (kin warning kin). Smell and hearing |
| **3. Pursuit** | **Legs** (speed), **wind** (sprint and stamina). Fat slows a body (drag) | The same legs and wind. A climbing escape over steep ground | **Agility** (turning, dodging) vs top speed. **Endurance hunting** (wearing prey down). Terrain choice (fleeing toward cover) |
| **4. Capture**: subduing | **Size:** the hunter must be 1.2× bigger. **Gape:** jaws suited to prey size. **Hunting build:** good gape and legs lower the size needed. **Pack:** jawed kin nearby help. **Crush:** big jaws beat armour. **Lunge:** a miss costs energy | **Armour** (needs minerals from limestone or the reef). Being **big**. **Weak** bodies (starving, old, sick) are easier to take. **Guard** (built, *not released*: kin near the prey make capture harder) | **Counter-attack:** horns, kicks or bites that hurt the hunter. **Mobbing** a hunter. Injury as a cost of hunting |
| **5. Handling**: eating it | **Gut:** meat digestion grows with gut. **Meat is poor:** a hunter keeps ~35% of a kill. Digestion takes time. **Resistance** to toxin | **Toxin** (from thicket shoots): a hunter is sickened, then **learns to shun that colour** (aversion) | **Hunting doesn't pay by skill** (flesh is not in "skill pays"). **Warning colours:** toxin is invisible until tasted, and only colour memory exists. Mimicry |
| **6. Life history** | Predators would live slower, with fewer and better young (not modelled by role) | Prey breed fast. **Parental care** (released at s2585: young stay near their mother). **Condition-dependent breeding** (released at s2583) | Litter size and care tied to role or body size (r/K). Breeding when food is plentiful |

## What this means
- **Hunting can't pay off today.** Even a well-built hunter gets ~35% of a kill, with no extra for skill, and must win stages 1–5. So killing happens mostly as **famine cannibalism**: starving bodies eat weak kin.
- **Prey defences are thin at detection and capture.** There's no camouflage, alarm or vigilance, and no counter-attack. Armour depends on minerals, and toxin on thicket. A rare new species has nothing that is *its own* way of surviving.
- **Diversity needs trade-offs.** Each defence should cost something (upkeep, eating time, speed), so different species pick different answers. For example, armoured and slow vs fast and light vs hidden and small vs toxic and coloured. That's how real communities hold many prey species.

## Proposal (Luis's choice; each tested on copies before going live)

| # | Change | Side | Cost / trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | **Hunting pays:** flesh joins "skill pays" (hunting build: gape fit plus legs), and a well-fitting kill keeps more energy | Hunter | Jaws and speed cost upkeep |
| **P1** | **Alarm calls:** a body that sees a hunter warns kin within range, and they flee | Prey | Calling draws the hunter's notice to the caller |
| **P2** | **Guard** (already built): kin near the prey cut capture | Prey | Groups compete for food |
| **P3** | **Vigilance:** keen-eyed bodies spot hunters sooner but eat a little less while watching | Prey | Less food |
| **P4** | **Horns / counter-attack** (new gene): a failed attack injures the hunter in proportion to the prey's horns | Prey | Horns cost upkeep and growth |
| **P5** | **Camouflage** (new gene): a body matching its ground's colour is seen from shorter range | Prey | Fixed to one kind of ground |
| H2 | **Ambush/stealth** (new gene): a hunter is seen from shorter range | Hunter | Slower movement |

**The steward's suggestion:** a first package of **H1 + P1 + P2**. H1 gives hunters a reason to exist, and P1 and P2 are prey's group answers, so the arms race starts balanced. Then **P3–P5 and H2** as a second package, which brings the anatomical diversity: horns, camouflage and stealth.
