## 1. In one paragraph
**The roadmap's whole effect.** All of the hunter roadmap went live in this window:
- skill (s1024), packkin (s1028), sizevar (s1032), massroom (s1036);
- the predator brakes (s1017) and the larger room (s1016).

**Predation doubled again:** 7,161 animals eaten in 31 seasons (3,410 the window before, 640 the one before that). Being eaten is now **the most common death, by far**.

The world settled into **one prey line and one hunter family**:
- **the prey:** the tytyvens;
- **the hunters:** the saomors, which divided four times (s1024, s1030, s1037, s1048), so their family now forms three species: moquioms, thuzunas, beltyzus.

Every other group was eaten out, **about 17 species in 31 seasons**, most within 4–9 seasons of being founded. Two things are new:
- **Size has split by role:** prey 5.0, hunters 7.6–8.4. The size spread went from 0.48 to 1.71, the largest in the family's history.
- **The hunters are also dung-eaters:** decomposer ability 0.41–0.44, with dung 16–27% of their diet. The world's predators are hunting decomposers that mostly graze.

## 5. How it shows in the creatures
- **Predators and prey are separating by size**, the change the roadmap aimed at. The prey line shrank from 6.9 to 5.0, small enough that the hunters (7.6–8.4) clear the size rule easily. Room by mass rewards it: five 5.0-size tytyvens take the room of three 8.4 thuzunas.
- **Wide mouths pay** (gape +0.58): the skill switch counts gape, and the hunters' mouths widened first.
- **The hunters are dung-eaters too.** The saomor line began as a decomposer split, and its daughters keep a decomposer ability of 0.41–0.44. In the drought, 207 animals lived mainly on dung, most of them hunters. Meat still comes in bursts (2% of diet on average), so they are **hunting decomposers, not carnivores**.
- **Browse came back** (+1.06): thicket shoots are the drought's food (winter buds and dry-season juiciness).

**Which rules and conditions drove the changes**
1. **The hunter roadmap as a whole:** valley, skill and packkin let hunters take healthy adults (the eaten had fat). Predation took 7,161, twice the window before.
2. **The great divide plus predation** is a turnover engine: each divide makes small groups, and the hunters eat them out. About 17 species came and went.
3. **Sizevar plus room by mass** let size split by role: the prey are small and many, the hunters big and fewer.
4. **The drought** (s1044–s1052) emptied fat stores and pushed everyone to dung and shoots. The hunters' decomposer ability carried them.
5. **The predator brakes** (pyramid, lunge) kept the family's numbers steady (358–399), though not the species count.
