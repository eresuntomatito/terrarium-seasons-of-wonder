## 3. Why niche bands never split (studies s2364–2370)
- **The bands are behaviour, not lineages.** A body keeps a "mixed feeder" label for 1.6 days on average, and "highland" for 1.5.
- **The label isn't inherited:** young share their parent's label at chance rates (19% against 15%).
- **The genes are the same across bands** (browse and climb alike), apart from a faint gut signal in scavengers and omnivores.
- So the niche rule was looking for groups the world never makes.
- **Luis chose option A: gene bands.** These are groups defined by inherited genes: meat-leaning guts, top climbers, swimmers, browsers, dung-eaters, insect-catchers and biters, and small or big bodies. They're far closer to the split bar (50–72% against 0–43%).
- A 12-season test is at season 9. So far: 70+ gene attempts, all blocked on the difference test, and no split yet.
