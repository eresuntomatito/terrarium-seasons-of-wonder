## 1. In one paragraph
**The world has new physics, a new stomach, and scrub on its open land, and the quilikes are answering.**
- **Swimming is the trait on the rise** (+0.79 SD), and so is **size** (5.40 → 5.59): M3's stomach ∝ mass favours big grazers.
- **Climbing is falling** (−0.65 SD). Under M2, a climb takes real time, during which a body can't eat. In this window the starved and the breeders alike climbed *more* than today's living (+0.43, +0.49), so the heights cost more than they pay. The highland band shrank from 40 to 26. That's the opposite of what M2 was meant to reward, and it's worth Luis's eye (see §7).
- **Browse is rising** (+0.53 SD). Since shrubland and open-scrub browsing, **a new way of life appeared: 17 mixed feeders** (grass plus shoots), and browse went from 0% to 6% of the family's diet. It's the first diet widening in many windows.
- Still one species.
