## 5. How it shows in the creatures
- **Legs** rose (+0.79). The range's slopes cost energy to climb, and long legs pay less. The galushtys, the species most at home among the streams, have the longest legs (0.68) and the most swimming (0.036).
- **Swimming** is stirring (0.006–0.036): selection may be starting to lift it in the lines that live by the water.
- **Coats** don't differ yet between the range (0.080) and the lowlands (0.085), since it's a warm era. The first cold era on full relief will be the test.
- **Dung** keeps rising (0.63): lean grass plus the mountains' streams and wet banks.
- **Hunting is shifting:** the sabelushs (802 kills) and navennas lead it; the omnivore splits didn't last.
